### 1. AI-Based Identification of Microbial Biomarkers Associated with Parkinson's Disease

**Master's Final Year Research Project – UM6P, Faculty of Medical Sciences**

- Development of a Machine Learning approach for identifying bacterial biomarkers associated with Parkinson's disease.
- Analysis of 16S rRNA metabarcoding data across multiple independent cohorts.
- Data preprocessing, feature selection, and microbiome data normalization.
- Development and evaluation of cross-cohort predictive models.
- Investigation of model generalizability and biological interpretability.
